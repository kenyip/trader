### MODEL — cost per paid mile

Let:

\[
C_{\text{paid}} = \frac{D + E + M + K + I + R + O}{m_{\text{paid}}}
\]

where  
\(D\) = depreciation, \(E\) energy, \(M\) tires/maintenance, \(K\) cleaning, \(I\) insurance, \(R\) remote ops, \(O\) platform/depot overhead,  
\(m_{\text{paid}}\) = paid miles per vehicle-year.

\[
D = \frac{V - S}{L},\quad
m_{\text{paid}} = 365 \cdot u \cdot h \cdot v \cdot p
\]

- \(V\) = vehicle + fit-out capex  
- \(S\) = residual  
- \(L\) = lifetime years (or use lifetime miles)  
- \(u\) = days available / 365 (uptime)  
- \(h\) = in-service hours / available day  
- \(v\) = mean speed while in service (mph)  
- \(p\) = paid-mile ratio (paid miles / in-service miles)

### Sensitivity table (MODEL inputs, not Tesla actuals)

Assumptions that are **not** company-measured: electricity $0.14/kWh; Cybercab 0.25 kWh/mi (**UNKNOWN**, Model Y is higher); tires+maint $0.06/mi; cleaning $0.04/mi; insurance $0.08–0.20/mi (wide because AV commercial rates are **UNKNOWN**); remote ops $0.05–0.25/mi; overhead $0.05/mi.

| V (capex) | Lifetime mi | Paid mi / yr | Util. story | D $/mi | E+M+K | I+R+O (lo/hi) | **C_paid lo/hi** |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $30k CLAIM | 300k | 40k | modest | 0.10 | 0.14 | 0.18 / 0.50 | **0.42 / 0.74** |
| $30k CLAIM | 300k | 80k | high | 0.10 | 0.14 | 0.18 / 0.50 | **0.42 / 0.74** |
| $45k (Model Y-like) | 250k | 40k | modest | 0.18 | 0.16 | 0.18 / 0.50 | **0.52 / 0.84** |
| $45k | 250k | 80k | high | 0.18 | 0.16 | 0.18 / 0.50 | **0.52 / 0.84** |
| $30k | 300k | 20k | low / empty miles | 0.10 | 0.14 | 0.18 / 0.50 | **0.42 / 0.74** + empty-mile leak |

Depreciation per mile is \(V/L_{\text{miles}}\) and **does not fall** when you drive more in a year — utilization instead amortizes **fixed remote/depot** and raises the chance you hit lifetime miles before obsolescence. The table’s \(D\) is per *lifetime* mile. **Fixed annual** remote/depot cost **does** fall with paid miles:

| Fixed opex / veh-year | Paid mi 20k | 40k | 80k |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $8,000 | $0.40 | $0.20 | $0.10 |
| $20,000 | $1.00 | $0.50 | $0.25 |

**INFERENCE:** Tesla’s advertised **$0.20/mi** opex (if that figure is still being used — treat as **COMPANY CLAIM / analyst echo**, not a 2026 measured print) only closes if **insurance + remote + cleaning** stay tiny **and** paid-mile ratio is high. Waymo-like utilization (~15–25 paid trips/vehicle/day on a mature metro) is the **economic** requirement. Tesla has not shown trip density. Q2 paid-mile deceleration argues they are **not** there yet.

**Break-even vs human rideshare:** U.S. Uber/Lyft all-in driver+platform cost is commonly modeled ~$1.0–1.5/mi in urban cores (**MODEL / industry**, not a Tesla filing). Tesla wins on paper **if** \(C_{\text{paid}} \lesssim \$0.60\) and fare ~$1/mi. That is a **conditional**, not a demonstrated result.

**Paid-mile ratio needed:** if empty reposition + charge + clean consume 40% of miles, you need ~1.7× more lifetime miles or lower V. **UNKNOWN** Tesla deadhead ratio.

## 6. What Musk is optimizing for

Read the Q2 2026 letter as a **system**, not a taxi press release.

1. **Owned fleet now, platform later.** First-responder plan: Tesla owns the cars **today**. IR outlook: Tesla will **allocate** hardware between customer sales and owned fleet. Owner-supplied “Tesla Network” is a **future capital-light story**, not the 2026 mechanism.
2. **Camera + data + Cortex, not lidar cities.** Same stack as FSD (Supervised). That is why CA still calls it Level 2 for consumer/Bay Area, and why NHTSA FSD probes matter to Robotaxi.
3. **Cybercab-specific miles** are the admitted bottleneck (Musk on the Q2 call). Manufacturing without that data does not unlock density.
4. **Energy + 4680 + pack capacity.** IR: battery pack capacity is “the main limiting factor to near-term vehicle production.” 4680 is explicitly tied to Cybercab and Semi.
5. **Compute / chips.** Cortex 2 ramping; Austin semiconductor fab “early stages.”
6. **Optimus adjacency.** Fremont S/X lines torn out for Optimus; same “real-world AI” pitch. Robotaxi is the **on-road** half of a physical-AI valuation.
7. **Service network / Superchargers** as depot infrastructure (cleaning, charge, recovery).

**Capital consequence:** Q2 FCF already negative on a $5.8B capex print. A Tesla-owned robotaxi fleet is **balance-sheet intensive**. If validation lags, Tesla is left holding purpose-built 2-seaters that cannot be sold as ordinary cars (no wheel). That is the **real** bottleneck cost — not the BOM slide.
